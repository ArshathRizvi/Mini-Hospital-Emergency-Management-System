"Next is Component 3: Treatment History managed using a custom Stack.
When doctor consultations conclude, completed treatment records are pushed onto 
the stack following the LIFO principle — Last-In, First-Out.

cases at the top.

Option 3 displays the treatment history in reverse-chronological order, showing 
the latest patient (ID 106) at the top.

If we perform a Pop operation (Option 2), the most recent treatment record is 
popped from the top of the stack, leaving the older record (ID 103) as the new top.
